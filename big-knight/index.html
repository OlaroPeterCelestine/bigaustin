A Time We Need It Most.

Every generation faces a moment that tests what a community is made of. This is ours.

Families are stretched. Small businesses are fighting to hold on. Neighborhoods that built this city are being priced out of it. And yet, in every corner of Central Texas, there are people who refuse to stand down. Mentors. Builders. Lenders of first chances. Defenders of the dream.

We call them Knights. And on one extraordinary night, we call them to the stage.

From BiG Idea Day to The BiG Knight

Get Ready for the BiG Reveal! This One's Worth the Wait.

Prepare for an unforgettable evening as BiGAUSTIN presents The BiG Knight Awards Gala at the world-renowned Circuit of the Americas.

The celebration begins with an exclusive VIP Red Carpet reception, followed by a lively general reception where guests can mingle, explore the silent auction and try their luck at games of chance.

From there, the evening unfolds with an elegant sit-down dinner and an inspiring program featuring a celebrated keynote speaker, live entertainment, and the Knighting Ceremony honoring the Community Knights whose work is transforming Central Texas.

When the paddles rise during our signature Raise Your Paddles giving moment, every hand in the room will help empower small businesses, families, and dreams across our community. uKnighted, we rise to empower.

Every Level Directly Powers

Every sponsorship dollar goes to work in Austin and Central Texas' underserved communities through BiGAUSTIN, the city's first federally designated CDFI, with a 30-year track record:

  • Community development initiatives that build generational wealth where it's needed most
  • Entrepreneur empowerment through microlending, business education, and one-on-one consulting that turns ideas into thriving enterprises
  • Affordable housing and sustainable communities that keep families rooted in the neighborhoods they call home
  • Workforce and youth development that prepares the next generation of leaders and business owners
