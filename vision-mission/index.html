By expanding fair access to capital, small-business resources, workforce tools, livable-wage opportunities, and affordable-housing support, BiGAUSTIN works to reduce economic disparities, advance social equity, and build shared prosperity for families and communities.

Our Mission Expanded

What We Do. Why It Matters.

BiGAUSTIN empowers individuals, entrepreneurs, and families through education, business consulting, access to capital, workforce development, and community-focused resources that create pathways to economic opportunity.

BiGAUSTIN fosters inclusive economic growth and improves quality of life for entrepreneurs, job seekers, and underserved Texans. Through financial empowerment, small-business education, individualized consulting, responsible capital, workforce development, community well-being initiatives, and affordable-housing support, BiGAUSTIN helps people overcome systemic barriers, build sustainable businesses, increase income, and create stronger communities.

How We Bring Our Mission to Life

Capital & Financial Opportunity

We expand access to responsible capital and financial resources for entrepreneurs who may face barriers to traditional financing, helping small businesses start, stabilize, and grow.

Business Education & Consulting

We provide practical business education, entrepreneurial training, and individualized consulting that help business owners build strong foundations, make informed decisions, and pursue sustainable growth.

Workforce & Economic Mobility

We connect individuals with education, skills, and resources that strengthen career pathways, earning potential, entrepreneurship, and long-term financial stability.

Housing & Community Development

We support initiatives that strengthen neighborhoods, expand access to quality affordable housing, preserve community assets, and help families build lasting economic security and generational wealth.
