Client Grievance Policy and Procedures

It is the policy of BiGAUSTIN to provide all clients with a copy of the Client Grievance Policy and an opportunity to file a grievance without adverse repercussions.

  1. Step 1: Contact the CEO/President to discuss your complaint or submit it in writing. A written decision is provided within 30 days.
  2. Step 2: Within 15 days, request a review by the Grievance Committee of the Board of Directors. A final written decision follows within 45 days.
