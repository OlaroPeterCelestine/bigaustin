OUR VISION - MISSION

Vision Statement

BiGAUSTIN provides fair access to capital, resources to build small businesses, tools to attain livable wages, and quality affordable housing.

Mission Statement

BiGAUSTIN's mission is to help families and empower underserved Texans by providing education, consulting, and capital. We foster small Business opportunities, facilitate Investment, and promote economic and community Growth through entrepreneur resources, microloans, workforce development education, and affordable housing support.
