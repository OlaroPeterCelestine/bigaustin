The BiGAUSTIN School of Business & Job Skills Development

At BiGAUSTIN, we empower individuals and strengthen our community. We provide essential resources and training for both job seekers and entrepreneurs, equipping them with the skills and knowledge to succeed in the dynamic Central Texas economy.

Our programs focus on practical steps to support a strong and growing small business community. Every day, BiGAUSTIN works to help Texans succeed through education, business training, and access to capital.

Our charge is to equip and inspire individuals to view entrepreneurship as:

  • An employment opportunity
  • An avenue out of poverty
  • A path to economic growth in low income and disadvantaged communities
  • A resource to help under-served small businesses succeed

Many individuals and businesses face barriers to success due to limited access to high-quality and affordable education and resources. BiGAUSTIN bridges this gap by providing essential support to both entrepreneurs and job seekers. We connect talented individuals with the resources they need to thrive, whether they are starting a business or seeking employment.

BiGAUSTIN offers a diverse range of programs, including seminars, workshops, and intensive training. Our approach is personalized, tailoring our support to the unique needs and goals of each individual. By providing comprehensive training in job skills and entrepreneurship, we empower individuals to build successful careers and contribute to a thriving local economy.
